Before you run them

Target iPhone, iPad and Mac only, for now

Google Play is still a closed test, so Android viewers who aren't testers can't install. Every ad says "Free on the App Store". Add Android ads after the public Google Play release.

Which size goes where

1:1 Facebook and Instagram feed. 4:5 feed on phones, the most space. 9:16 Stories and Reels: text stays clear of the top 14% and bottom 20%, where Meta puts its own buttons.

Videos

MP4, H.264, 30 fps, 8 to 11 seconds, under 1 MB each. Silent, with every message on screen; you can add music from Meta's sound library in Ads Manager.

Keeping it true

Smart Redact "finds personal details for you to review". Never say it anonymises automatically or give an accuracy figure. Smart Redact, Nexa Magic and Remove Watermark are Pro, so their ads carry the PRO tag.

Changing the kit

Ad text and links: marketing/ads/ads.json. Images and videos: node marketing/ads/make-ads.cjs. Then npm run verify, commit and push to main.
