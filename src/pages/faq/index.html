FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what NexaScan does, and what it doesn't.

Privacy

Does NexaScan upload my documents?

No. Scanning, clean-up, text recognition, conversion and the PDF tools all run on your phone, and the app never uploads your documents, images or their text. A few small exceptions, none of which includes your documents: the app downloads the current Pro plans and Free limits; if you buy Pro or redeem a Pro code, it checks your Pro status with our licence service (and, for a purchase, with the App Store or Google Play); and on Android only, Google's text-recognition component may send limited diagnostic data to Google, and if you turn on usage data or crash reports, which are off by default, technical data goes to Google's Firebase. The iPhone app has no usage analytics or crash reporting at all. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Every tool works without an Internet connection, including text recognition, whose model is built into the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore NexaScan Pro, and now and then so the app can confirm that your subscription has renewed.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start scanning.

Where are my files stored?

In NexaScan's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't browse. This storage is left out of iCloud backup on iPhone and Google cloud backup on Android. To keep a copy elsewhere, share or save the file to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email.

Scanning and text

Does NexaScan use AI, and where does it run?

Yes, four AI models, and all of them run on your device. The Nexa Page Model labels each part of a page (paper, text, handwriting, lines, pictures, creases and stains) for Nexa Renew, Nexa Magic, No Shadow and Remove Handwriting. The Nexa Watermark Model helps Remove Watermark find the marks to clean. The Nexa Redact Model helps Smart Redact find personal details for you to review. Text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Google's ML Kit model on Android. All four work on the device and offline, so your pages are never sent anywhere to be processed. Page detection and the other looks use image processing that also runs on your phone.

Does it support OCR (text recognition)?

Yes. NexaScan recognises text in scans, photos and PDFs on your device. On Free you can extract the text, up to 10 pages at a time, and search scanned PDFs of up to 10 pages in the Document Viewer. Pro adds searchable PDFs, lifts the search limit and adds the OCR Document Viewer, where you select and copy text straight off scanned pages. Recognition reads Latin-alphabet languages, such as English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.

Can I create searchable PDFs?

Yes, with NexaScan Pro. When you save a scan as a PDF, turn on Make PDF searchable. For an existing scanned PDF, use the Make Searchable PDF tool. The page looks the same, but its words can now be found and copied in any PDF reader.

Can I scan several pages into one document?

Yes. Keep scanning to add pages, then reorder, rotate, retake or remove them before you save. Free saves up to 10 pages per document; Pro allows up to 200 pages in one scan.

Does NexaScan add a watermark to my files?

No. Your files never carry a NexaScan watermark or logo, on Free or Pro.

Files and conversion

What file types are supported?

NexaScan opens PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG files. It creates PDFs, JPEG images, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files, and with Pro, one long image of every page. Password-protected PDFs open in the Document Viewer once you enter the password, but other tools can't use them.

Can I convert a PDF to Word or Excel?

Yes, from a PDF or a photo. On Free, Word takes up to 50 pages at a time and Excel up to 10; Excel puts tables on sheets with numbers and dates kept as values. Pro adds Word Pro and Excel Pro, with editable text and tables that follow the original layout and complex tables more closely. Complex pages may still come out arranged differently, so check important files.

Will a tool change my original file?

No. Merging, compressing, protecting, converting and the other tools always create a new file. Your original is left as it was.

How do I protect a PDF with a password?

Password protection is part of NexaScan Pro. Open Password Protect, choose the PDF and enter a password of at least 8 characters. NexaScan saves an encrypted copy using AES-256. The password isn't stored anywhere, so keep it somewhere safe: it can't be recovered.

Free, Pro and purchases

What can I do for free?

Scan documents, clean them up with five looks, recognise and extract text, sign, merge, split, compress, organise pages, and convert to Word, Excel and images. Free jobs have limits: 10 pages per document or job (50 for Word), 3 files per merge and 50 MB for compression.

What does Pro add?

Pro lifts the Free limits and adds Nexa Magic, Nexa Renew and five more clean-up looks, Remove Watermark and Remove Handwriting, searchable PDFs, the OCR Document Viewer, Word Pro and Excel Pro, Edit PDF with shapes, form marks and a saved signature, watermarks, page numbers, multi-page sheets, one long image, Smart Redact, and password protection. It's a monthly or yearly subscription, through the App Store on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Google Play on Android. See Pricing.

Does Pro carry over between Android and iPhone?

No. The App Store and Google Play are separate stores. A subscription from the App Store unlocks Pro on iPhone, and on your other Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account, up to a limit. A subscription from Google Play unlocks Pro on Android. To have Pro on both, you would need a subscription from each store. A Pro code works on either.

How do I restore purchases?

Your subscription is tied to the store account you bought it with: your Apple Account on iPhone, or your Google account on Android. On a new phone, sign in to the App Store or Google Play with the same account, open NexaScan, go to Settings › NexaScan Pro and tap Restore purchases. If Pro still doesn't appear, see Support.

I have a Pro code. How do I use it?

Go to Settings › NexaScan Pro, tap Have a code?, type the code and tap Redeem. Codes work on iPhone and Android. You need an Internet connection to redeem one. Pro from a code lasts until the code's end date.

How do I cancel on iPhone?

Open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose NexaScan and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from the app: on the NexaScan Pro screen, tap Manage subscription in the App Store. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For a refund, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I cancel on Android?

In the Google Play app, go to Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose NexaScan and cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Redacting and anonymising

How do I hide personal details in a PDF or photo before sharing it?

Open Smart Redact (Pro) and pick the file, or scan one. Choose what to look for (names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, dates of birth, card and bank details, passport and ID numbers, and any words of your own), then review what it found: keep anything visible, confirm the items marked "Please check", and draw a box over anything it missed. Choose a box, a label such as [NAME] or a pseudonym such as Person A, and save. You get a new copy; your original is not changed.

Is the hidden information really gone?

Yes, on the pages you redact. Those pages are saved as flattened images with the boxes burned in, so the text, pictures and layers under a box are removed, not just covered, and can't be copied back out. Pages with nothing hidden keep their text. Document details such as author and title are removed from the copy.

Does Smart Redact find everything?

It finds personal details for you to review, and it marks the items it is unsure of, but no tool can promise to catch everything. Always check each page before you share, and draw a box over anything it missed. It reads English, with UK and US formats for numbers, addresses and dates, and it doesn't read handwriting.

Is my document uploaded to find the personal details?

No. Text recognition, detection and redaction all run on your device, and work offline. Nothing about the document is sent anywhere.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

Is NexaScan on iPhone?

Yes. NexaScan is on the App Store as NexaScan: PDF Scanner & Editor, for iPhones running iOS 16 or later. It is the same app as on Android, with the same tools and the same Free and Pro plans.

Can I use NexaScan on an iPad or a Mac?

Yes. The same App Store app runs on iPads with iPadOS 16 or later, and on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later), where you get it from the Mac App Store and it opens in its own resizable window. On a Mac you can scan with its camera, or open PDFs and photos from your files. A Pro subscription from the App Store works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhones and iPads running iOS or iPadOS 16 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later), from the App Store, and Android phones running Android 8.0 or later, from Google Play. On Google Play, NexaScan is available as a test version for invited testers until its public release.

Is anything different on iPhone?

The tools are the same. Two things work differently underneath: text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on iPhone and Google's ML Kit on Android, both on the phone; and the iPhone app has no usage analytics or crash reporting at all, while on Android you can choose to share them. Pro is bought through the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android.

Is it suitable for all ages?

Yes. NexaScan is a document tool with no ads and no account, and it is rated 4+ on the App Store, which means suitable for all ages.
