Privacy Policy

Your documents are yours. This page explains, in plain language, what NexaScan does with your data and what it doesn't.

Effective {{config:policyDate}}. Applies to the NexaScan app for Android.

The short version

  • Scanning, clean-up, text recognition, conversion and every PDF tool run on your phone.
  • NexaScan never uploads your documents, images or the text in them, to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • Your documents are kept in the app's private storage and are left out of Google cloud backups. They leave the app only when you share them or open them in another app.
  • If you buy NexaScan Pro or redeem a Pro code, the app checks your Pro status with our licence service (and, for a purchase, with Google Play), first when Pro starts and then again from time to time. The licence service keeps the purchase token and the code only as one-way hashes, with a random identifier for your installation. Nothing it keeps identifies you: no name, email address, Google account or documents (section 5).
  • Google's text-recognition component (ML Kit) may send limited diagnostic data to Google. It never includes your images or text (section 4).

2. What the app works with on your phone

  • Camera images, when you scan a document.
  • Files you choose: PDFs and images (JPG, JPEG, PNG) you pick with Android's file picker, or send to NexaScan from another app with Open with or Share. A PDF you open in NexaScan from another app is kept as an unchanged copy in its Documents until you delete it.
  • The documents it creates: PDFs, images, Word and Excel files, and the text it recognises in them. Word and Excel files record the name of the file they were made from as their title.
  • Passwords you type in Protect. They are used once to encrypt the new copy, and are never saved or logged.
  • A few app settings, such as whether you've seen the introduction and the app tour.
  • If you buy Pro or redeem a code: the Pro status the app has confirmed (where your Pro comes from, when it ends and when to check it again), and the private half of the installation key, which never leaves your phone (section 5). A code you type is sent to the licence service to redeem it and isn't saved on your phone.

All of this stays on your phone, and we never receive it. If your phone's Google backup is turned on, Android may include the app settings in that backup, but never your documents (section 8).

3. What we collect

We don't collect your documents, images, recognised text, file names or contacts, and NexaScan has no advertising or analytics SDK. The only data that reaches us is the licence data described in section 5, and only if you buy NexaScan Pro or redeem a code.

5. NexaScan Pro and the licence check

Buying Pro

NexaScan Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card or payment details.

After a purchase, the app confirms the subscription with our licence service so that Pro can be unlocked, kept active and restored. While you use the app, it confirms the subscription again about once a week, so that renewals, cancellations and refunds reach your phone. For this, the app sends:

  • the purchase token that Google Play issues for your subscription. The licence service uses it to check the subscription with Google Play, and stores it only as a one-way hash, which can't be turned back into the token;
  • an installation identifier, made from a random key that the app creates in your phone's secure key store, together with a signature proving the request comes from this installation. It isn't based on your phone's serial number or other hardware IDs, your phone number or your Google account.

Redeeming a code

We give free Pro codes to Google Play's app reviewers, to testers, and to people we choose to give Pro as a gift or for promoting NexaScan. Codes are never sold. When you redeem one in Settings › NexaScan Pro, the app sends the code with the same installation identifier and signature described above.

The licence service stores codes only as one-way hashes. For your installation, it records a grant: which code it came from, the name we gave that code (such as "Google Play review"), when you redeemed it, when it ends, and whether we withdrew it. The names we give codes describe what they're for and never name a person. While you have Pro from a code, the app checks your Pro status with the licence service more often, when you open the app or a Pro tool, so that a new end date or a withdrawn code reaches your phone.

If we send you a code by email or message, we use your address only to send it. The licence service doesn't record who received a code.

What the licence service keeps

The licence service keeps only the hashed purchase token, the installation identifier with its public key, and the state of the subscription: the plan, when it started, when the paid period ends, and whether it renews. For a code, it keeps the hashed code and the grant described above. It doesn't receive or store your name, email address, Google account, payment details, IP address, documents or their text, and you don't need an account. It runs on Amazon Web Services in the United Kingdom (London region).

We keep these records while your subscription is active, and afterwards so that Pro can be restored on this phone or a new one. Grants from codes are kept after the code ends, as the record of how each code was used. Because these records contain nothing that identifies you, we can't find them from your name or email address. Uninstalling NexaScan deletes the key on your phone, so that installation identifier can't be used again. The licence service's own logs are deleted automatically within 30 days.

If you never buy Pro or redeem a code, the app doesn't contact the licence service at all.

6. Data sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. When you tap Share or Open, Android passes the chosen file to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies. The licence data in section 5 is processed by Amazon Web Services on our behalf, and Google Play handles purchases under its own terms.

8. Where your documents are stored

  • Documents you save are kept in NexaScan's private app storage. Other apps can't read this area, and it is protected by your phone's own security and encryption.
  • This storage is excluded from Google cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, a direct device-to-device transfer to a new phone may copy it. The installation identifier and licence information are excluded from both. App settings, such as whether you've seen the tour, can be included in Google cloud backup.
  • Working files are kept in the app's private cache while a task runs and are deleted afterwards. Leftover temporary files more than a day old are removed the next time you open NexaScan.
  • NexaScan doesn't save documents to your gallery or shared folders. They leave the app only when you share or open them.

9. Retention and deletion

Your documents stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete any document in the app, and uninstalling NexaScan or clearing its storage in Android Settings removes all its documents and settings. We hold no copy of your documents. The licence service holds no name, email address or account, so we can't look up a person's licence record (section 5). For any question about it, email {{email:privacyEmail}}.

10. Security

  • Processing happens on the phone, so your documents don't travel over the Internet when you use NexaScan.
  • The licence check and code redemption (section 5) travel over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Protect (Pro) encrypts a new copy of the PDF with AES-256. Your original file is unchanged, and your password isn't stored. The Protect screen blocks screenshots while you type.
  • NexaScan doesn't add its own encryption to documents in its storage. They rely on Android's app sandbox and device encryption. For sensitive files, use Protect.

11. Children

NexaScan is a general-purpose document tool. It isn't directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
