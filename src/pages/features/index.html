The details

Supported files and limits
OpensPDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG. Files can also arrive from other apps through Open with and Share. Password-protected PDFs open in the Document Viewer once you enter the password; other tools can't use them.
CreatesPDF, JPEG images, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx). Pro adds one long image of every page.
Text recognitionRuns on your phone and works offline. It reads Latin-alphabet languages, such as English, French, German and Spanish.
Password protectionPro. AES-256 encryption on a new copy of the PDF. Your password is not stored.
Your originalsTools create a new file. The file you started from is left unchanged.
Free limits10 pages per scanned document (also the most Sign PDF and Crop & Enhance will open), 50 pages per Word conversion, 10 pages or images per Excel conversion, text-recognition or import job, 3 files per merge, and PDFs up to 50 MB for compression. Pro lifts these limits.
For everyoneUp to 200 pages in one scan and files up to 500 MB. In one go, Extract Text reads up to 10 pages, Word conversion takes up to 50, PDF to Images saves up to 20 (10 in one long image), and Remove Watermark and Remove Handwriting take PDFs of up to 100 pages and 50 MB, and check up to 25 pages at once.
PlatformAndroid phones running Android 8.0 or later.
