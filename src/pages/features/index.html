The details

Supported files and limits
OpensPDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG. Files can also arrive from other apps through Open with and Share. Password-protected PDFs open in the Document Viewer once you enter the password; other tools can't use them.
CreatesPDF, JPEG images, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx). Pro adds one long image of every page.
On-device AIText recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Google's ML Kit model, built into the app, on Android. Nexa Magic, Nexa Renew, No Shadow and No Handwriting use NexaScan's own page model, and Smart Redact its own redact model; both run on your device.
Text recognitionWorks offline. It reads Latin-alphabet languages, such as English, French, German and Spanish.
Password protectionPro. AES-256 encryption on a new copy of the PDF. Your password is not stored.
Smart RedactPro. Finds personal details for you to review; it does not promise to find everything, so check each page before you share. It reads English, with UK and US formats for numbers, addresses and dates. Redacted PDF pages are saved as flattened images with the boxes burned in, so nothing remains under a box; pages with nothing hidden keep their text. Document details such as author and title are removed from the copy.
Your originalsTools create a new file. The file you started from is left unchanged.
Free limits10 pages per scanned document (also the most Sign PDF and Crop & Enhance will open), 3 text boxes and 20 pen strokes per scanned document, 50 pages per Word conversion, 10 pages or images per Excel conversion, text-recognition or import job, 3 files per merge, and PDFs up to 50 MB for compression. Pro lifts these limits.
For everyoneUp to 200 pages in one scan and files up to 500 MB. In one go, Extract Text reads up to 10 pages, Word conversion takes up to 50, PDF to Images saves up to 20 (10 in one long image), and Remove Watermark and Remove Handwriting take PDFs of up to 100 pages and 50 MB, and check up to 25 pages at once.
PlatformsiPhones and iPads running iOS or iPadOS 16 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later), from the App Store, and Android phones running Android 8.0 or later, from Google Play. The tools, looks and Free limits are the same on all of them.
