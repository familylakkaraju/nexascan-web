How it works

A safe copy in four steps

Smart Redact is an assistant you stay in charge of: it finds, you decide.

  1. {{icon:scan}}

    Pick or scan

How to hide

Hidden your way, and hidden for real

Pages you redact are saved flattened, with the boxes burned in: the text, pictures and layers under a box are removed, not just covered, so nothing can be copied back out. Document details such as author and title are removed too.

Questions

Smart Redact, answered

All questions and answers

Is my document uploaded?

No. Text recognition, detection and redaction all run on your device, and work offline. Nothing about the document is sent anywhere.

Does it find everything?

It finds personal details for you to review and marks the items it is unsure of, but no tool can promise to catch everything. Check each page before you share, and draw a box over anything it missed.

Is the hidden information really gone?

Yes, on the pages you redact. They are saved as flattened images with the boxes burned in, so what was under a box can't be copied back out. Pages with nothing hidden keep their text.
