Questions

Good to know

All questions and answers

Does NexaScan upload my documents?

No. Everything you do to a document happens on your phone. The Privacy Policy lists the few things the app does send, such as checking your Pro status, and none of them includes your documents.

Is it only a scanner?

No. Besides the scanner, NexaScan has {{gen:tool-count}} tools for editing, signing, converting, organising and protecting PDFs and photos. See every tool.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Every tool works without a connection, including text recognition and the clean-up looks. You only need one to buy or restore Pro, and now and then so the app can confirm that your subscription has renewed.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or later.
