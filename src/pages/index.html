Questions

Good to know

All questions and answers

Does NexaScan upload my documents?

No. Everything you do to a document happens on your phone. The Privacy Policy lists the few things the app does send, such as checking your Pro status, and none of them includes your documents.

Is it only a scanner?

No. NexaScan is an AI document studio. The scanner is where many documents start, and besides it NexaScan has {{gen:tool-count}} tools for restoring, editing, signing, converting, organising, redacting and protecting PDFs and photos. See every tool.

Can I hide personal details before sharing a document?

Yes, with Smart Redact (Pro). It finds names, addresses, phone numbers, account and ID numbers and more in a PDF or photo, for you to review. You choose what to hide, then it saves a new copy with those details covered and the text under them removed. It runs on your device, so the document is never uploaded.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Every tool works without a connection, including text recognition and the clean-up looks. You only need one to buy or restore Pro, and now and then so the app can confirm that your subscription has renewed.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhones and iPads running iOS or iPadOS 16 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later), from the App Store, and Android phones running Android 8.0 or later, from Google Play, where NexaScan is available as a test version for invited testers until its public release.
